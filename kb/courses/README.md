# Course digests

Digests of HB Network playlists, written by Ivan Neto from the lesson transcripts and the
public code, in Portuguese, keeping each digest's section numbering so citations like
`section: "12.9"` resolve here.

They derive from Henrique Bastos's lectures and are published with his permission, all rights
reserved, until he confirms a license in writing. They are not under the CC BY 4.0 that covers
the rest of `kb/`.

Quotes in these digests were captured from automatic transcripts and not checked against the
video; treat every one as `verified: false`.
