**Rule.** End every work conversation on an action with a date. Ask so that the only possible
answer is an action, and when nobody knows the date yet, agree on the date by which it will be
known. Be assertive about this, and kind.

**Why Henrique says so.** Effective communication demands that the action continue (Método para
Cumprir Qualquer Prazo, lesson 6, 0:02:58 to 0:03:10): ask so the only answer is an action
(0:06:18 to 0:06:36), always with a date, or with the date on which the date will be known
(0:11:47 to 0:12:11; 0:14:38 to 0:14:51, `verified: false`, transcript paraphrase). Without a date
you are last in the queue (0:17:35 to 0:17:49), and without a deadline there is no project
(0:19:03). It is assertive but kind and generous (0:19:44 to 0:20:24).

**Looks like.** "Can you send me the API credentials by Thursday? If you can't know by then, when
will you know?" Bad: "let me know when you have the credentials"; a meeting that ends on "we'll
look into it".

**How to detect.** Judgment: requests, tickets or meeting notes that end without an owner and a
date; blockers waiting on someone with no agreed date to follow up.

**How to fix.** Rewrite the closing question so its answer is an action and a date; when the
answer is "I don't know", ask for the date of knowing. Related: `discuta-o-problema-nao-o-prazo`,
`liderar-e-remover-obstaculos`.
