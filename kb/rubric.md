Nota mecânica formula: penalty(principle) = files with a finding of that principle divided by max(files in scope, 5), so one bad file costs at most a fifth of a dimension; penalty(dimension) = weighted mean over its mechanical and partial principles whose rules were applicable; score(dimension) = 10 * (1 - penalty), one decimal; overall = weighted mean of the scored dimensions. Judgment-only principles never enter this number. The weight column below sums every principle of the dimension; the CLI report shows the applicable weight, which sums only the mechanical and partial principles whose rules had a chance to fire. Weakest dimension: lowest score, ties broken by higher weight then name. The formula lives here so it can be read, not inferred.
